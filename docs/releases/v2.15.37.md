## Service dependency review

Before changing selected service startup types, Optimaxer reads and lists their direct dependencies and dependents in the existing confirmation window. Load-order groups are identified, and disabling a service with direct dependents is flagged. If the service manager cannot return dependency information, no confirmation is offered and no service is changed. Existing protected-service checks, start-type backup and restore behavior remain in place.
