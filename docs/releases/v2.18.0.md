# v2.18.0

Six Linux-distribution themes and more motion in the desktop app. The PowerShell and Linux editions are unchanged apart from the aligned version number.

## New in the desktop app
- **Linux distribution themes**: Ubuntu Yaru (aubergine and orange), GNOME Adwaita, KDE Breeze, Linux Mint, Pop!_OS and Manjaro, each with a dark and a light version and a preview in Settings. GNOME Adwaita and KDE Breeze show the Overview as a compact list; Pop!_OS uses the ring-gauge dashboard. They are colour themes inspired by those desktops, not copies of their artwork. All pass the same contrast checks as every other theme.
- **More motion**: cards fade in one after another when you open a page, a card's border warms toward the accent while the pointer is over it, Overview numbers on the dashboard and list layouts count to their new value instead of jumping, and theme previews glow on hover. Nothing loops, and every animation is skipped when reduced motion is on (Settings, or Windows' animation setting).

## Limits
- The themes change colours, shapes and the Overview layout; other pages keep their layout.
- Motion was checked in screenshots of the finished state, so how it feels on your PC is yours to judge.
